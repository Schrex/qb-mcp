# QuickBooks in Claude: Jeri's sheet

Claude can look things up in the Company File while QuickBooks is open on your PC. It only reads; it never changes anything. Type questions in plain English, using names exactly as QuickBooks shows them.

## Things you can ask

Swap in your own Vendor, Customer:Job or dates.

1. "Which Bills from Patton Hardware Supplies are still unpaid?"
2. "Is Bill 930357 from Patton Hardware Supplies already entered?"
3. "What do we owe Vendors right now, oldest Bills first?"
4. "Which Bills were charged to Abercrombie, Kristy:Remodel Bathroom this year?"
5. "What are the costs and income on Abercrombie, Kristy:Remodel Bathroom?"
6. "How does Abercrombie, Kristy:Remodel Bathroom compare to its estimate?"
7. "Which Customer:Jobs are in progress?"
8. "How profitable are the Customer:Jobs in progress this year?"
9. "What card charges went on Burch, Jason:Room Addition this year?"
10. "How many hours went on Acme:Main St Paving in March?"

## What Claude can't do

- **Change anything.** No entering, editing, paying or deleting Bills or anything else. Do that in QuickBooks.
- **Attach files.** Attachments stay with QuickBooks' Attach File button.
- **Payroll.** No paychecks or pay rates. Hours on a Customer:Job, yes; what anyone is paid, no.

## Reading the trust stamp

Every answer carries a short note of what it is based on. Ask "show me the trust stamp" if Claude leaves it out.

- **Basis:** accrual (counted when entered) or cash (counted when paid). Lists and Bills say basis does not apply.
- **Dates:** the date range the answer covers, or "as of today" for balances.
- **Closing Date:** "closed" means those dates are locked. **"May still change"** means the dates run past the Closing Date, so new or edited entries can still move the figures.
- **Filters:** the Vendor, Customer:Job, dates or status the answer was limited to. Check these match what you asked.
- **Voided:** voided transactions are listed but never added into a total; the stamp says how many.
- **Check in QuickBooks:** the report or screen to open to check the answer, e.g. Reports > Vendors & Payables > A/P Aging Detail.

## "Totals incomplete"

If the stamp says *"Showing the first 100; more exist; totals incomplete"*, Claude only saw part of the list, so there is **no total**, and any total you work out from it is wrong. Ask again, narrower: one Vendor, one Customer:Job, or a shorter date range (e.g. one month). Repeat until the notice is gone.

## Before you rely on a figure

Open the "check in QuickBooks" report for the same dates and filters and compare. If they differ, trust QuickBooks and tell Sumanth.
